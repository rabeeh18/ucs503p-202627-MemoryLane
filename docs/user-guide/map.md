# Library Map

**Map** in the header opens a picture of everything you've saved. Pages are dots, grouped into **topics** drawn as labelled ovals. A bigger oval means more pages.

The map needs at least 10 saved pages. With fewer, it asks you to save a few more.

## Moving around

- **Scroll** to zoom, **drag** to pan.
- **Fit** (bottom left) brings the whole map back into view.
- **Find a topic…** searches topic names and the pages inside them, and zooms to the best topic.
- **List** shows the same topics and pages as a list.

In large libraries, page dots stay dim until you zoom in, so the topics stay readable.

## Topics

Click a topic to open its panel:

- The topic's pages
- **Search only these pages**, which runs a search inside that topic

Hovering over or selecting a topic shows the links between its pages. Dashed lines between ovals join topics that are about similar things.

Pages that don't fit any topic well sit between the ovals, smaller and dimmer, and show as **Not in a topic**.

## Pages

Click a dot to open its panel:

- Title, site and when you saved it
- **Open page**
- **Summarise**
- **Related pages**: your other saved pages closest in meaning to this one

The selected page's links to its nearest pages are highlighted.

On a phone the panel opens as a sheet from the bottom of the screen.

## When the map changes

Saving or deleting a page rebuilds the map the next time you open it. Topic names are kept when a topic's pages stay the same.

See [Library Map Architecture](../architecture/map.md) for how topics and their names are made.
